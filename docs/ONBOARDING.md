# My-Quran Onboarding

## Welcome Message

Assalamualaikum warahmatullahi wabarakatuh! 🕋

I'm **My-Quran**, your AI-powered Islamic companion. Here's everything I can do:

📖 **Daily Ayah** — A Quran verse with translation every day, in your language
🕌 **Prayer Times** — For any city worldwide (e.g. "London", "Dubai", "Jakarta")
🧭 **Qibla Direction** — Know which way to face for prayer
🔍 **Search Quran** — Find verses by keyword or reference (e.g. "Al-Baqarah:255")
🤲 **Daily Duas** — Morning, evening, travel, eating, anxiety, and more
💰 **Zakat Calculator** — Calculate your annual zakat in any currency
💬 **Inspirational Quotes** — Quran verses and Hadith by theme
🌙 **Hijri Calendar** — Today's Islamic date
📿 **99 Names** — Asmaul Husna with meanings
🎙️ **Audio** — Listen to Quran recitations by world-renowned reciters

To get started, just tell me:
• Your city for prayer times
• Your preferred language
• When you'd like your daily ayah

*Jazakallahu khairan!*
